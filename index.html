      Neon Static

      NEON STATIC

      Rainline Market, up to 5v5. Ghostwire attacks and plants the spike; Helix defends and purges it. Sides swap at halftime.

      Match

      Changing these restarts the match.

      How a round works

      • Buy phase (12 s, frozen): press B in spawn. SMG 1250, shotgun 1050, rifle 2700, scoped DMR 4750, armour and four kinds of throwables.
      • Attack: carry the spike to site A or B and hold E for 3.2 s. It goes off after 40 s.
      • Defend: kill all attackers, run out the 1:45 clock, or hold E on a planted spike for 7 s.
      • Economy: wins pay 3250–3500, losses 1400 rising to 3400, kills 300 (more with SMG, shotgun and knife). Dying loses your gear.
      • Throwables: EMP blinds, smoke blocks sight, fire burns an area, the decoy fakes gunfire.

      Practice range

      • Test Range 07 (pick it under Map): one-tap heads at 10–45 m, a strafe yard with peekers, and jump boxes.
      • Counter-strafe: tap the opposite key to stop. The speed meter shows your stop time.
      • Spray: hold fire on a wall and pull down, then left, right and left again.
